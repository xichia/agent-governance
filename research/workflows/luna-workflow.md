# Luna Workflow

1. **Plan:** Define objective, constraints, acceptance criteria, and dependencies.
2. **Implement:** Delegate scoped work to Luna with context and expected outputs. Parallelize independent tasks; avoid overlapping edits.
3. **Review:** Have a separate Luna check correctness, completeness, and edge cases. Validate.
4. **Iterate:** Fix and recheck until criteria pass.

Own coordination, integration, and final verification. Report outcome, validation, and limitations.
