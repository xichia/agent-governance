Structured safe unknown only means null answer fields and complete=false; supporting prose can still be wrong. Every reason and limitation therefore requires separate manual review against the frozen evidence. The independent review must check this limitation rather than treating structured agreement as prose correctness.

They test grading/provenance behavior and are not additional model observations.
