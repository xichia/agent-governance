## What changed in knowledge, and what mostly grew

The most consequential new knowledge is the preservation of concrete counterexamples and protocol deviations: X01’s residual Unicode URL acceptance; X04-06’s wrong-scope false negative, wrong service sequence, and pointer/lookup representation deviations; plus the distinction between local bytes served and agent-reported reading. These alter what can responsibly be claimed and keep an owner decision open. The early rehearsal also surfaced useful design questions but did not resolve real-world service or accessibility assumptions.

Much of the intervening package growth is engineering confirmation: schemas, hashes, frozen manifests, fixture runners, broker events, replayable grading, snapshots, and review trails make claims inspectable and preserve history. That is valuable for reproducibility and recoverability. Repeated status predicates across 02–06 and 64/256 row counts should not be summed as equivalent units of learning: later packages reuse earlier families and fictional authorities, and 05/06 carry material scripting, worker and presentation confounds. The source cards are descriptive inputs, not new weekly developments. Artifact count is an audit/burden proxy only; no cost, efficiency, topology winner, runtime-model verification, general reliability, or production-readiness result is available.
