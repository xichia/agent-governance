# Science, art and shared wonder — actual Luna rehearsal result

This proposal was produced by four separately dispatched Luna specialist sessions during a local rehearsal. All venues, suppliers, prices, availability and donated staffing are **fictional**. No event was booked, promoted or operated. Independent acceptance state and evidence are in [T-GOV-010](../../records/tasks/T-GOV-010.md); this document is a proposal, not legal/access clearance.

## Proposed event

- Fictional event date: 14 November 2026, Australia/Perth.
- 80 adults; public programme 18:30–20:30. Setup 18:00–18:30 and pack-down 20:30–21:00.
- Science talks, observation through art, conversation and light refreshments.
- Fixed seating plan: 60 Hall / 20 Quiet Room, with the quiet option hosted throughout the public programme.
- Fictional budget ceiling: AUD 6,000.00 including modeled taxes, fees and 10% contingency.

## What changed and what remains open

The v1 proposal cost AUD 5,665.00 and used the covered courtyard. Under the disclosed fictional CH-001 closure, all four roles acknowledged brief v2. The courtyard add-on was removed, reducing total cost by AUD 330.00 including contingency. The workshop moved indoors. Earlier quiet-host timing and chair-transfer conflicts were addressed at the schedule/planning level; actual layout and staffing remain unverified.

A human would still need to confirm the room layout and accessible routes, safe refreshment pickup/occupancy and seating arrangements, ingredient/allergen information, actual volunteer availability and all real-world permissions before any operational use. All advisory mitigations remain proposed.
